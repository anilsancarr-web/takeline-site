# Takeline

Takeline is a teleprompter for iPhone that records the video while you read.
The script scrolls over the camera preview; a thin reading line sits just
under the lens so your eyes stay on it; and the recorded file contains only
what the camera saw — the words are drawn beside the capture, never into it.

## How a take works

1. Write a script, or paste one in. The editor shows how long it reads at a
   natural pace.
2. Point the phone at yourself and tap Record. Speed and text size are live
   controls; tap the script to pause and it resumes at the same word.
3. Keep the take, save it to Photos, or delete it. Nothing is uploaded and
   there is no account.

## What it costs

Takeline is free to download and to look around. Writing and recording need
Takeline Pro, bought once or by the year; both unlock the same features. The
yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
