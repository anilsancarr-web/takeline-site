# Privacy Policy — Takeline

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

Takeline has no account and no server of its own. This page answers the
questions a teleprompter that records video actually raises.

## Does the script end up in my video?

No. The script is drawn over the camera preview as a separate layer; the
recording is written by the camera from what the sensor sees. The file never
contains the text. This is how the app is built, not a setting.

## Where do my recordings go?

To your iPhone. When you choose "Save to Photos", the take goes into your own
photo library — on your device or in your own iCloud Photos under your Apple
Account — never to us. A take you delete on the review screen is removed from
the device. We have no copy and no way to obtain one.

## Where do my scripts go?

Nowhere. Scripts are written and stored on the device, along with your
settings (speed, text size, opacity, countdown, default camera). There is no
sign-up, no login and no profile. We could not retrieve your scripts if you
asked us to.

## What does Takeline send anywhere?

**Only what purchases need — and that happens every time the app opens,
whether or not you buy anything.** Purchases go through Apple's App Store and
are checked through RevenueCat, the service Takeline uses for them:

- **Each time Takeline opens**, and again when you come back to it after a
  while, it asks RevenueCat whether Takeline Pro is active on this phone and
  which plans are on sale.
- **When the paywall opens**, it loads those plans with their prices and, if
  the yearly plan has a free trial, checks whether your Apple Account can
  still take it.
- **When you buy Pro, start a free trial or tap "Restore purchases"**, Apple
  handles the transaction and the receipt is validated through RevenueCat.

These requests carry a randomly generated identifier, created on the phone the
first time Takeline opens and not linked to your name or your Apple Account.
They also carry basic device information, including the iPhone model, the iOS
and app versions, your preferred languages and the identifier iOS gives the
app's maker for this phone (not the advertising identifier), plus your App
Store country and, after a purchase or a free trial, the receipt. Like any
server, RevenueCat also sees the IP address a request comes from.

RevenueCat keeps a record under the random identifier. Takeline uses the
answer for one thing, knowing whether Pro is active, and keeps the last answer
on the phone so that Pro still works when you open the app offline. This is
also what makes "Restore purchases" work on a new phone. RevenueCat's privacy
policy is at <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>.

RevenueCat handles this data for Takeline as a service provider, under the
Data Processing Addendum that is part of its terms
(<https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa/>). It may process the data only on our
instructions and must protect it with appropriate security measures: the same
protection this policy promises. The record is kept while Takeline uses
RevenueCat and is deleted when that ends, or earlier if you ask (see "Your
rights" below).

Your scripts, your recordings and the settings you choose in the app are never
part of these requests.

Opening a link on this page, the Terms and Privacy links in the app, or the
Rate and Contact rows in Settings, hands you to Safari, the App Store or Mail.
Those are Apple's apps and Apple's policies apply once you are there.

## What do the camera and microphone permissions cover?

- **Camera** shows the preview under the script and records the video. iOS
  asks before the first take.
- **Microphone** records audio with the video. iOS asks first; if you decline,
  Takeline records silently rather than refusing to work.
- **Photos** is requested only when you choose to save a take, and only for
  adding to the library. Takeline does not read your photos.

You can withdraw any of these in iOS Settings at any time.

## Is there any tracking?

No. No analytics SDK — no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK. No
advertising, no ad identifier, no IDFA, no tracking across apps. No crash
reporting that sends your content anywhere. No cloud sync, no backup to our
servers, no account recovery — none of these exist. We do not sell or rent
personal data, and we share none beyond the purchase checks described above.

## Children

Takeline is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content, no ads, no chat
and no user-to-user features. Apart from the purchase checks described above,
it collects nothing from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Data-protection law gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete
your personal data. Takeline keeps nothing about you on any server of ours:
your scripts, settings and any takes still inside the app are on your phone,
and deleting the app removes them, together with the random identifier.

The one record outside your phone is the RevenueCat record described above.
To ask what it contains, or to have it deleted, email us at the address
below. If you bought Pro or started a free trial, give the date and your App
Store country so we can find it; we then delete it in RevenueCat. Deleting it
does not cancel a subscription, which you manage in your App Store settings.

Apple keeps its own record of every purchase and trial. Apple's policy is at
<https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.
