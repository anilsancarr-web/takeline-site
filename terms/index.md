## What the app does

Takeline shows text over the camera preview and records video with the
device's own camera. The overlay is drawn beside the capture, not into it, so
the script does not appear in the recorded file.

## Takeline Pro

There is no free version of Takeline. You can download the app and look
around it without paying, including the script list and Settings; writing a
script, opening one and recording all need Takeline Pro. If a trial or a
yearly subscription ends, your scripts stay on the phone and can still be
deleted; opening one needs Pro again.

Takeline Pro includes recording in 1080p and 4K with no watermark, takes of up
to an hour, unlimited scripts, text mirroring for a beam-splitter rig, video
mirroring for selfie takes, a custom countdown, tap-to-pause, the reading line
under the lens, and speed, text size and opacity controls. It is sold two
ways, and both unlock the same features:

- **Lifetime** — a one-time purchase, charged to your Apple Account by Apple
  when you confirm it. Nothing renews and nothing is charged again.
- **Yearly** — an auto-renewable subscription that starts with a 7-day free
  trial for new subscribers, described below.

Both are charged at the price shown for your country. Pro is tied to your
Apple Account; "Restore purchases" in Settings re-applies it on a new device
at no cost.

## Yearly subscription

The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. When the
trial ends, the yearly price is charged to your Apple Account unless you
cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. The subscription then renews
automatically every year at the same price unless you cancel it at least 24
hours before the current period ends.

The trial is an introductory offer: each Apple Account can have it once, and
the App Store decides who is eligible. If your Apple Account is not eligible,
for example because it has already had the trial, the yearly plan starts
without one and the first year is charged when you confirm the purchase.
Takeline mentions the trial only when the App Store reports your Apple Account
as eligible, and Apple's purchase sheet shows whether it applies before you
confirm.

You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in your App Store
subscription settings, or from "Manage subscription" in the app's Settings.
Cancelling stops the next charge; Pro stays active until the end of the trial
or of the period already started. If the price ever changes, Apple notifies
you in advance and, where required, asks for your consent before the new price
applies.
